My research develops wireless sensing systems that connect communication signals with physical-world perception. I combine signal processing, optimization, learning, prototype implementation, and real-world experiments to advance integrated sensing and communication (ISAC), low-altitude drone sensing, and passive mmWave sensing.

## Representative Work

### mmAlert: Joint Device Localization and Target Tracking

**Problem.** Can a communication system simultaneously localize transmitters and reconstruct a passive target's trajectory without dedicated radar signals?

**Approach.** We developed *mmAlert*, a cooperative passive sensing system that extracts angle-of-arrival and bistatic Doppler measurements from 60-GHz communication links. An alternating-optimization framework jointly estimates device locations and target trajectories, while an extended Kalman filter efficiently shapes the trajectories.

**Results.** On a 60-GHz prototype, mmAlert achieved average transmitter-localization and trajectory-reconstruction errors of 0.07 m and 0.20 m, respectively, when observations from diverse trajectories were available.

### FOCUS: Drone-Enabled Outdoor Transmitter Localization

**Problem.** How can a single low-cost drone localize an unknown outdoor transmitter without infrastructure support?

**Approach.** FOCUS equips a drone with two spatially separated receive antennas and uses the Doppler-frequency difference generated by drone motion. Coherent detection cancels carrier- and sampling-frequency offsets, allowing the time-varying Doppler difference to reveal the transmitter location.

**Results.** Experiments with a commercial Wi-Fi transmitter achieved average localization errors of 0.49 m under rotational flight and 0.79 m under translational flight.

**Links.** [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/LWC.2026.3712042) · Code: coming soon · Dataset: coming soon

### Passive Handwriting Tracking with Weak mmWave Signals

**Problem.** Can weak communication signals reconstruct fine-grained hand motion, even without line-of-sight paths?

**Approach.** We designed a cooperative passive sensing framework with one transmitter and two receivers. The system fuses Doppler measurements across receivers to estimate handwriting trajectories from weak mmWave communication signals.

**Results.** The system achieved millimeter-level handwriting reconstruction: 90% of trajectory errors were below 6 mm, including in non-line-of-sight settings.
