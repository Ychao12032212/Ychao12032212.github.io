I am a Ph.D. candidate in Control Science and Engineering at the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech).

My research lies at the intersection of wireless communications and sensing, with a focus on integrated sensing and communication (ISAC), low-altitude drone sensing, and Wi-Fi sensing and standards. I develop sensing algorithms and end-to-end prototypes, and validate them through real-world experiments.

## Research Interests

- Integrated sensing and communication (ISAC)
- Low-altitude drone sensing and passive localization
- mmWave sensing, localization, and target tracking
- Wi-Fi sensing, WLAN standards, and resource scheduling
- AI for wireless sensing and sim-to-real adaptation

## Highlights

- Developed **mmAlert**, a 60-GHz ISAC prototype for joint signal-source localization and passive target tracking. Real-world experiments achieved localization errors of 0.07–0.76 m and trajectory-tracking errors of 0.20–0.29 m.
- Proposed a drone-enabled dual-antenna FDoA localization approach, achieving 0.49–0.79 m localization error with commercial Wi-Fi devices.
- Developed passive handwriting reconstruction with weak mmWave communication signals, with 90% of reconstruction errors below 6 mm.
- Worked with Huawei, HONOR, ZTE, and National Instruments on 6G communications, Wi-Fi standards, and sensing systems.
